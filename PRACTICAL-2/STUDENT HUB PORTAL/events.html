Benefits of Participating

  • Improve technical and practical skills.
  • Develop teamwork and leadership qualities.
  • Build confidence and communication skills.
  • Win certificates, medals, trophies and prizes.
  • Meet students from different departments.
  • Learn from faculty members and industry experts.
  • Strengthen your resume and portfolio.
  • Create memorable college experiences.

Registration Process

  • Select your preferred event.
  • Complete the registration before the deadline.
  • Receive confirmation from the organizer.
  • Carry your Student ID card on the event day.
  • Attend the event and follow the schedule.

Event Guidelines

  • Carry your Student ID card.
  • Reach the venue at least 15 minutes early.
  • Follow the instructions of the organizers.
  • Maintain discipline throughout the event.
  • Respect judges, guests and fellow participants.
  • Keep the campus clean and protect university property.
  • Enjoy the event and encourage fair participation.
