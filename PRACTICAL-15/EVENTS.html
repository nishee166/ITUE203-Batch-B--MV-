College Events

Welcome to the Events section of the Student Hub Portal. Explore technical, cultural and sports events organized for students at CHARUSAT.

Benefits of Participating

  • Improve technical and practical skills.
  • Gain real-world experience through competitions and workshops.
  • Develop teamwork and communication skills.
  • Showcase your talent and creativity.
  • Build networking opportunities with students and professionals.
  • Receive certificates and recognition for participation.

Registration Process

  • Select the event you want to participate in.
  • Check the event date, time and venue.
  • Complete the registration process.
  • Provide the required student information.
  • Submit your registration before the deadline.
  • Attend the event on the scheduled date.

Event Guidelines

  • Carry your valid college identity card.
  • Reach the venue before the scheduled time.
  • Follow the instructions provided by the organizers.
  • Maintain discipline and proper behavior during the event.
  • Do not damage college property or event equipment.
  • Follow all safety and security instructions.
