About Student Hub Portal

Student Hub Portal is an educational website created to help students in their learning journey. It provides study materials, coding practice, project ideas, hackathon details, event information, and student portfolios on one platform.

The main purpose of this portal is to make learning simple, fast, and organized. Students can improve their knowledge, prepare for exams, participate in competitions, and develop technical skills using different learning resources.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to provide students with quality educational resources and create a platform where they can learn, practice, participate in hackathons, and grow together.

🚀 Our Vision

Our vision is to build a smart learning community where every student can improve their academic, technical, and programming skills with the help of technology.
