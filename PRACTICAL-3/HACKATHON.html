Benefits of Participating

  • Improve programming skills.
  • Build teamwork and leadership.
  • Learn new technologies.
  • Win certificates and prizes.
  • Build an impressive portfolio.
  • Meet industry experts and mentors.
  • Improve communication skills.

How to Participate

  • Select a hackathon.
  • Register before the deadline.
  • Create a team if required.
  • Develop your project.
  • Submit your solution.
  • Present your project to the judges.

Tips for Success

  • Understand the problem statement.
  • Plan your project before coding.
  • Manage your time effectively.
  • Test your project carefully.
  • Prepare a clear presentation.
  • Work together with your team.
