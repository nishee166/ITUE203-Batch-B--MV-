Hackathons

Explore exciting hackathons and innovation challenges available for students. Search, filter and sort hackathons to find the perfect opportunity to showcase your skills.

Why Participate in Hackathons?

  • Improve programming and problem-solving skills.
  • Work on real-world problems and innovative ideas.
  • Build impressive projects for your portfolio.
  • Learn teamwork and collaboration.
  • Connect with students, developers and industry experts.
  • Gain experience useful for internships and placements.

Hackathon Preparation Tips

  • Understand the problem statement carefully.
  • Choose a suitable technology stack.
  • Divide responsibilities among team members.
  • Build a simple working prototype first.
  • Test your project before submission.
  • Prepare a clear project presentation and demo.
